## About

Product engineer with 6 years shipping software people actually use, across
mobile, web and the services behind them. Based in Doha, Qatar.

Right now I lead 5 of 10 front-end engineers at **Commercial Bank of Qatar**,
working on corporate internet banking, digital account opening and trade
platforms — regulated environments where security review and audited releases
are part of every change.

Before that I built the **OTTAA Project** from zero to a six-figure user base as
its sole front-end and back-end engineer, and drove its Digital Public Good
certification end to end.

I like problems that run from the database to the pixel, and I care about the
part most people skip: what happens when it is live, busy and someone is
depending on it.
